# Method

## Decision Criteria

Assess source diversity, primary evidence, independent pickup, community or repository momentum, novelty, and plausible impact area.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Read each event, estimate momentum from signal counts, estimate novelty from what changed, identify impact areas, set trend score, choose watchlist recommendation, and explain the decision.

## Scoring or Classification Rules

Scores above `0.75` require strong evidence and multiple signals. `track` is appropriate for notable or novel events with continuing signals. `escalate` requires high score and strong operational impact.

## Edge Cases

Single-source marketing claims may be novel but should not become high momentum without corroboration. Breakthrough claims should stay `breakthrough_claim` until independently supported.
