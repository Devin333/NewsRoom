# Method

## Decision Criteria

Evaluate publisher type, original evidence, author/date completeness, reputation hints, and signs of promotional or rumor-based language.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Start with the publisher type, map it to an initial tier, inspect available metadata, add risk flags, and calibrate the score to the amount of direct evidence.

## Scoring or Classification Rules

Official blogs, verified repositories, and research platforms can score above `0.9` when the content is direct and complete. Community and social posts should remain below primary-tier scores unless they provide original evidence.

## Edge Cases

An official source with heavy marketing language can still be primary but should carry a risk flag. A high-reputation media outlet covering an official announcement is trusted secondary unless it provides original reporting.
